Exercise:
You have access to the Northwind database, which contains information about
orders, customers, products, and suppliers. 

Your task is to analyze supplier performance for the year 2016.
Specifically, identify which suppliers provided the most orders and calculate
the total number of items ordered from each supplier. 
Only include suppliers who provided more than 10 orders in 2016. 
The results should include the supplier's contactname, the total number of orders,
and the total quantity of products supplied.

Hint steps:

Table Joins:

Use JOIN to link the relevant tables (Suppliers, Products, OrderDetails, Orders) together.
Subqueries:

Use aggregate functions like COUNT() and SUM() to calculate the total number of orders and total items ordered for each supplier.
GROUP BY:

Group the results by SupplierName to aggregate the order data for each supplier.
HAVING:

Use the HAVING clause to filter suppliers with more than 10 orders.
WHERE:

Use WHERE to filter for orders within a specific date range (in this case, for the year 1997).
Ordering Results:

Use ORDER BY to sort the results based on the total number of orders.
